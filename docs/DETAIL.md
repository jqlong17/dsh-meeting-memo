Open Meeting Minutes in the right sidebar.

Live is for the meeting happening now: mark the start, end it when you finish, and that span becomes the selection. Today shows the 06:00–24:00 speech density; drag to select a range and read its transcript, scroll to zoom, drag the bar underneath to pan. Minutes lists each finished meeting with its background, key points, decisions, and to-dos; the writing style is yours to edit. Background holds project, people, and term cards you can tick when generating.

Capture and transcription run locally; model and recording settings live under Transcribe. Turning the plugin off stops capture and drafting; finished minutes and background cards stay on this machine.
